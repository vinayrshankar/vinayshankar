About

Researcher, developer, educator.

My work combines scientific questions with hands-on software development. I build tools when an experiment, laboratory workflow, or applied problem needs something more flexible than an off-the-shelf solution.

I am especially interested in software that makes research easier to reproduce, easier to audit, and less dependent on one-off scripts.

Principles
  • Document assumptions and limitations.
  • Prefer reusable configuration over hard-coded study logic.
  • Protect raw/source data from destructive workflows.
  • Preserve provenance in generated outputs.
  • Make tools understandable to the people who actually use them.
